Near-neighbour distance distribution (the tail that sets a cutoff).
The MAX is not usable at scale: pct_div is edits/core_len, so a pair
whose ends-free alignment leaves a tiny core reports ~0% divergence
while sharing nothing. At 1e8 pairs enough of those exist to pin the
max at 1.0 for every screen, which is what happened on the full MiSeq
run. Use the quantiles.
